Corrections/qualifications are explained and marked with dark-red erratum:
- FINAL AUDIT CORRECTION: the former 6.2.9 allegation about manual p.82 was
  false. Fresh visual inspection shows that the manual correctly prints -1,-1;
  the false erratum has been removed from the solution.
- 6.2.6: actual manual p.81 has final coordinate zero in the eigenvector for
  eigenvalue 2. It must be 1/sqrt(3); direct residual and normalization prove it.
- 6.2.15: manual calls the form positive definite and the surface an
  ellipsoid/ellipse. It has eigenvalues 0,1,3 and is an elliptic cylinder.
- 6.4.2: the orthonormal eigenvectors must be a complete n-vector basis.
- 6.4.3: nonsymmetry forbids real orthogonal diagonalization. It does not
  forbid complex unitary diagonalization unless real eigenvalues are added.
- 6.4.6: determinant wording is finite dimensional; the kernel/inverse
  implication still makes sense without an infinite-dimensional determinant.
- 6.4.7: equal spectra must include multiplicities.
- 6.4.9: zero denominator/zero off-diagonal cases of tan(2 theta) are handled.
- 6.5.4: the conjugate eigenvalues are real for integer multiples of pi.
- 6.5.10: the adjoint spectrum is conjugated; matched labels require
  lambda_i != lambda_j for cross-eigenvalue biorthogonality.
- 6.5.11-12: repeated singular-value vectors require an orthonormal choice;
  completeness and orthogonality, not just unit norms, justify reconstruction.
- 6.5.13: original TeX reversed first-column entries. Source p.326 has (2,1)
  divided by sqrt(5). Source part(c) has g_n instead of f_n on its RHS.
- 6.5.14: +1,-1 are paired-equation factors, not ordinary eigenvalues.
  This correction makes the problem coherent despite the manual's dismissal.
- 6.5.15: converses require the property for all a in a neighborhood of zero
  with H fixed. A verified non-Hermitian fixed-a counterexample is included.
- 6.5.16: every eigenvector survives exponentiation, but extra ones may appear.
- 6.5.17: eigenvalues belong to {0,1}; either value may be absent.
- 6.5.19(b): the secular equation determines eigenvalues, not eigenvectors.
- 6.5.21: Eq.(3.37) is passive. U actively rotates +120 degrees about
  (sqrt(2),0,1), while its coordinate axes rotate -120 degrees about that axis.
