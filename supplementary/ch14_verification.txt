Additional corrections and necessary qualifications, dark red in ch14.tex:
- 14.1.1: real-argument hypothesis for square bounds; the complex identity
  does not turn complex squares into nonnegative numbers.
- 14.1.7: J0 coefficient normalization needs continuity at its isolated zeros.
- 14.1.11: consecutive strictly positive roots; the origin is excluded.
- 14.1.14: manual x^2 C_n' should be x C_n' in the modified Bessel ODE.
- 14.1.15 and 14.5.3: noninteger Schlaefli contours are open Hankel
  contours around the negative-axis cut, not arbitrary closed circles.
- 14.1.16: hint e^(+/- i x) must be e^(+/- i pi).
- 14.1.20: a=0 needs b nonzero; the integral is then conditional.
- 14.1.22: the 83.8% energy estimate assumes a large aperture and a
  paraxial diffraction plane. The manual reverses a/lambda; the textbook
  calls 2 pi a/lambda=57120 small. Physical accessible angles are bounded.
- 14.1.24: omega=v k; the m=0 angular factors are one mode, and k=0
  contributes no nonzero clamped-membrane eigenfunction.
- 14.1.25: positive J_m' roots, not J_m roots. Manual separated factors
  omit z and the oscillatory i. The scalar constant transverse branch
  exists, but Maxwell divergence and side-wall flux exclude a TE mode.
- 14.1.26: ordinary cutoff frequency includes c; strict propagation is
  above cutoff, with zero group velocity at equality.
- 14.1.27: the three smallest mode families change order with aspect ratio.
  +/-m degeneracy is counted as one family; r=0 is an infinite-length limit.
- 14.2.2: the nu=0 Neumann basis includes the constant zero mode.
- 14.2.5: positive derivative roots alone are incomplete for the full
  stated range nu>-1. Include the constant at nu=0 and the unique negative
  eigenvalue I_nu mode for -1<nu<0; its norm is derived. Endpoint equality
  for prescribed nonzero Dirichlet data is an interior/L2 interpretation.
- 14.2.7: Parseval requires convergence in weighted L2; complex norm
  Parseval uses absolute squares, distinguished from the bilinear identity.
- 14.2.9: 24.558 is not the correctly rounded check value; the independent
  calculation gives 24.558563970889615..., hence 24.559 to three decimals.
- 14.3.3: manual J1,J0' should be Y1,Y0'.
- 14.3.5: manual second product needs plus; the combination is minus
  W[J_nu,J_-nu], giving the stated positive constant.
- 14.3.7 and 14.4.6: ordinary derivatives of the real oscillatory integral
  do not converge and the undamped boundary primitive does not vanish.
  An upper-half-plane/Abel argument is provided before taking the boundary.
- 14.3.10: f(0) is undefined and its zero limit is not an eigenvalue.
  Ratio poles are separated from roots; the annular determinant and k=0
  harmonic solution prove this distinction.
- 14.4.7: sine-transform identity is initially for x>0; oddness prevents
  the unchanged extension to negative x. The value at zero is a limit.
- 14.5.5: m=0 sine coefficient is arbitrary and conventionally set to zero.
- 14.5.12: textbook Eq.14.114 needs 1/rho, not 1/rho^2; the radial
  right side in Eq.14.116 needs delta(r-b)/r. The weighted jump fixes A=-1.
  Literal unweighted source would give -b; the printed inverse-square
  source would give -1/b. Positive k, source b>0, and k=0 cases are stated.
- 14.5.13: real integral requires y>0; negative y uses |y| and y=0 is
  a limiting transform, not substitution into K0(0).
- 14.6.1: manual saddle locations +/-x should be +/-1.
- 14.6.4: a leading logarithmic coefficient alone leaves an arbitrary I0
  addition; the constant ln 2-gamma is independently derived to fix it.
- 14.6.6: the drawn endpoint contours need Re nu>-1/2 and the unbounded
  positive-real contour also Re z>0; an endpoint primitive condition alone
  does not establish convergence or legitimate differentiation.
- 14.6.8: +i H0^(1)/4 gives a negative unit delta. The book's Chapter 10
  L G=+delta convention needs -i/4, verified by the disk flux and manual.
- 14.6.12: the lower angular endpoint is -pi/2; the exterior-right region
  has Re w>0, not <0, agreeing with Fig.14.15. The claimed contour
  impossibility pertains to admissible descent paths, not all analytic
  deformations. An explicit upper path and its constant phase are given.
- 14.6.13: x_min=6 means absolute error on the prescribed integer grid.
  Relative error in both amplitudes first meets the tolerance at x=8.
  These seven samples establish no continuous-real threshold.
- 14.7.3: besides the published odd j_n power correction, the second y_n
  sum requires a minus in the printed phase convention. The n=1 printed
  plus fails the ODE, and the finite Hankel polynomial proves the fix.
- 14.7.11: equal orders need the removable limit 1/(2 nu), nu>0.
- 14.7.12: manual denominator m^2-n^2 should be (m-n)(m+n+1).
- 14.7.14: standard Fresnel arguments are sqrt(2/pi)t, not sqrt(pi/2)t.
  After s=t^2, the functions are X(sqrt(s)),Y(sqrt(s)); delete the stray
  x in the printed second integral. Finite derivative telescoping retains
  its terminal terms, then proves convergence before taking the limit.
- 14.7.15: constant Neumann zero mode exists; the requested minimum is
  the lowest positive oscillation frequency. A Rayleigh bound excludes
  every l>=2, not just a finite numerical scan.
