Corrections/qualifications marked with the existing dark-red erratum macro:
- 4.1.7: missing grammatical "of" in the original note.
- 4.1.9: the complex-coordinate Lorentz representation is complex orthogonal.
- 4.1.10: the line-element argument needs symmetric metric coefficients;
  polarization determines only the symmetric part of a quadratic form.
- 4.2.1: numerical epsilon symbols use orthonormal Cartesian coordinates.
- 4.2.2: uniqueness is for the full antisymmetric tensor/vector correspondence.
- 4.2.3 and 4.3.12: continuous second derivatives justify commuting partials.
- 4.2.6: proper orthogonal rotations only; reflections reverse the ordinary
  epsilon matrix, as verified by the explicit reflection counterexample.
- 4.4.1: regular local functional-dependence hypotheses; singular critical
  points and a vacuous zero relation cannot support the unqualified argument.
- 4.4.2: |J|=h1*h2 generally; J=h1*h2 requires positive orientation.
- 4.4.3: y>0, since x/y is undefined at y=0.
- 4.5.1: textbook p.236 must count negative metric factors in the INPUT form,
  not the complementary form. All of Eq.(4.82)'s results are then correct.
- 4.7.1: invertibility is required for an actual change of coordinates.
- 4.7.3: local angular potential is arctan(y/x), not the manual's tan(y/x);
  the angular form is not globally exact on the punctured plane. The complex
  form needs holomorphic f for closedness and a primitive/vanishing periods
  for global exactness; simply connected domain is a sufficient condition.
